About the brand

doba.objects is a jewellery studio in Sarajevo, built on lost wax casting. Solid silver, solid ebony wood. No plating, no shortcuts.

Novo doba is molded, so it repeats and stays part of your everyday system. Limited and Bold are made to order. I design and make every one of those pieces myself, one at a time, and I never remake the same piece twice.
