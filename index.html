Studio structure and production

How doba.objects designs, makes, and delivers each piece.

doba.objects operates as a small independent studio producing wearable objects. All design and production take place in Sarajevo under the direction of a single maker.

The studio works across three collections:

  • Novo doba Molded pieces in solid silver. These designs are repeatable and made to order.
  • Limited Collection Pieces in ebony wood or wood combined with silver. Each piece is designed and produced individually.
  • Bold Collection Larger sculptural pieces produced individually, one at a time.

No collection uses plating. All metal is solid. All wood is solid.

Ordering and production time

All orders are produced individually. Production time depends on the collection and the current studio schedule. An estimated production window is communicated at the time of order.

Custom work and commissions

The studio accepts a limited number of custom commissions each year. Scope, price, and timing are agreed in writing before work begins.

Material clarity

All pieces are either solid metal, solid wood, or a combination of solid metal and solid wood. The studio does not use plating or hollow forms to imitate weight.

Care Guide

How to maintain solid metals and solid wood over time.

Solid metal

doba.objects metal pieces are solid throughout, not plated. Metal develops a natural patina through normal exposure to air and moisture.

To clean

Use a soft polishing cloth for light tarnish. For deeper tarnish, use a standard polishing cloth or mild metal cleaning solution.

To store

Keep pieces in a dry place away from direct humidity when not being worn.

To avoid

Avoid chlorine, perfume, and household cleaning chemicals applied directly to the piece.

Solid wood

Wood pieces are solid wood, not veneered or laminated. Grain, density, and tone vary naturally from piece to piece.

To clean

Wipe gently with a dry, soft cloth after wear. Avoid water and soap.

To store

Keep away from prolonged direct sunlight and extreme heat, cold, or humidity.

To avoid

Do not submerge in water or wear while swimming or showering.
