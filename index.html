Studio structure and production

How doba.objects designs, makes, and delivers each piece.

doba.objects operates as an small independent studio producing wearable objects. All design and production take place in Sarajevo, under the direction of a single maker. Pieces are produced using lost wax casting and hand finishing.

The studio works across three collections:

  • Novo doba (Base Collection) Molded pieces in solid silver. These designs are repeatable and available on an ongoing basis and made to order. They form the core, everyday system of the brand.
  • Limited Collection Pieces in ebony wood or wood combined with silver. Each Limited piece is designed and produced individually. Once a Limited piece is sold, that specific design is not repeated.
  • Bold Collection Larger, sculptural pieces that sit at the intersection of jewellery and object. Each Bold piece is designed and produced individually, one at a time. Pieces can be made to order via inqiry but will never be exactly the same..

No collection uses plating. All metal is solid; all wood is solid.

Ordering and production time

All orders are produced individually.

  • Novo doba pieces are made to order and produced in same base shape and finished by hand, with possibility of customizing texture and finishes.
  • Limited and Bold pieces are produced as individual works, some one of one and some made to order.

Production time depends on the collection and the current studio schedule. An estimated production window is communicated at the time of order. Customers are notified if any timing changes are required.

Custom work and commissions

The studio accepts a limited number of custom commissions each year.

For commissions, scope, price, and timing are agreed in writing before work begins. Because materials and studio time are committed to a single piece, a non-refundable deposit may be required at the start of the project.

Material clarity

All pieces are either:

  • Solid metal
  • Solid wood
  • A combination of solid metal and solid wood

The studio does not use plating or hollow forms to imitate weight. Surfaces age as the material itself, not as a coating.

Natural variation in wood grain, minor casting marks, and hand finishing are part of the studio’s aesthetic and are not treated as defects.

Returns and adjustments

The studio’s approach reflects the nature of its collections:

  • Novo doba pieces may be eligible for return or exchange within a defined period if unworn and in original condition.
  • Limited and Bold pieces, and commissioned work, are produced individually for a specific client and are generally treated as final sale.

Sizing adjustments for rings or chains are considered case by case, depending on the design and material constraints.

Full legal terms, including eligibility for returns and repairs, are outlined in the Terms and Conditions.

Care and longevity

Pieces are designed to be worn and kept.

A detailed care guide explains how to maintain solid metals and solid wood over time, including cleaning, storage, and environmental considerations. Customers receive this guide with their order and can consult an online version as needed.

Care Guide

How to maintain solid metals and solid wood over time.

Solid metal

doba.objects metal pieces are solid throughout, not plated. This means the material you see is the material you’re wearing, all the way through.

Metal develops a natural patina over time through normal exposure to air and moisture. This is not damage. It’s the metal aging the way it is meant to age.

To clean

Use a soft polishing cloth for light tarnish. For deeper tarnish, a standard polishing cloth or mild metal cleaning solution restores shine without affecting the metal.

To store

Keep pieces in a dry place, away from direct humidity, when not being worn. A small airtight pouch or box slows down natural tarnishing.

To avoid

Chlorine, perfume, and household cleaning chemicals applied directly to the piece. Remove jewellery before swimming, showering with strong products, or cleaning the house.

Solid Wood

Wood pieces are solid wood, not veneered or laminated. Grain, density, and tone vary slightly from piece to piece. This variation is the material, not a flaw.

To clean

Wipe gently with a dry, soft cloth after wear. Avoid water and soap, since wood absorbs moisture differently than metal.

To store

Keep away from prolonged direct sunlight, which can dry and lighten wood tone over time. Store in a stable, moderate environment, avoiding extreme heat, cold, or humidity swings.

To avoid

Submerging in water, wearing while swimming or showering, and prolonged contact with lotions or oils, which can seep into the grain.

Occasional care

A small amount of natural, unscented oil (such as mineral oil, applied sparingly with a cloth) can help maintain the wood’s depth of color if it appears to be drying out. This is optional and only needed occasionally.

Pieces combining metal and wood

Follow both sets of guidance above for the relevant material on each piece. Avoid soaking or submerging the full piece even briefly, since metal and wood require different handling.

A general note

Nothing here is plated, so nothing here wears down to reveal a different material underneath. What ages is the material itself, the same way it would in any solid object made to be lived with. Wear it, let it age, and it will keep telling you the truth about what it’s made of.
